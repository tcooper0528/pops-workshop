The story behind the workshop

I've spent my life at the crossroads of faith, storytelling, and kids — as a children's pastor, a church leader, and a children's librarian. These days I add one more ingredient: technology.

Everything here starts the same way: something I wished existed for my own grandkids — Xena, Gotham, and Harvey — or for the ministry leaders and parents I've served alongside for decades. If it makes a kid smile and plants a seed of Scripture, it belongs in the workshop.
