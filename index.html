About Mr. Tim

Hi, I'm Mr. Tim

I'm a grandfather, a former children's pastor and a librarian who writes faith-filled picture books about the friends of Maple Hollow and beyond.

My stories are made for reading aloud on a grandparent's lap, or for sharing the wonder of a child who is just beginning to read and learn new words. Through them I share gentle lessons about kindness, hope and the greatest light of all: love.
